In Australia, Centurion has established a strong presence in the purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA) sector through its Dwell portfolio, including Dwell East End Adelaide (300 beds) near the Adelaide University, and Dwell Village Melbourne City (611 beds) close to RMIT University and the University of Melbourne.

# Moving In Guide

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/moving

"Welcome to your new chapter at EPIISOD. We're so excited to have you! To make your move-in day smooth and stress-free, here's everything you need to know before you settle into your new home."

Check-In Process
1. Arrival & Greeting: When you arrive, head to the Main Lobby where our team will be waiting to greet you. We'll guide you through the check-in process and answer any questions you have.
2. ID & Booking Verification: Have your photo ID (passport or driver's licence) and booking confirmation ready for verification. This helps us make sure everything matches your reservation details.
3. Payment & Bond Confirmation: If you haven't already, make sure your rental payment and RTA bond are completed before move-in. Our team can help you check your payment status on the spot if needed.
4. Key Collection: Once verified, you'll receive your room keys or access card. Please keep it safe — replacement fees may apply for lost keys.
5. Room Inspection: Head up to your new room and take a few minutes to inspect the space. If you notice anything that needs fixing or cleaning, just let our team know right away through the EPIISOD Portal.
6. Settle In & Explore: You're all set! Unpack, make yourself at home and explore your new surroundings.

Important Safety Reminders: "At EPIISOD, your safety and comfort are our top priorities. Please help us keep our community secure and enjoyable for everyone by following these guidelines. If you're ever unsure or need assistance, don't hesitate to approach our friendly staff — we're always here to help."
- Avoid Damage: Please don't stick or hang anything on the walls to prevent marks or damage.
- Electrical Safety: All electrical items must stay under 1,200 volts to avoid tripping safety switches.
- Fire Safety: For everyone's safety, charging e-scooters or bikes inside your room is not permitted.

More move-in information (check-in times, documents, luggage, condition report, what to bring, resident apps and keyless access) is in faqs.md under MOVE-IN and LIVING at EPIISOD. Pre-arrival: after booking, residents receive a pre-arrival checklist email with required documents, tips and final steps ("Your room is reserved. # Booking Process (as described on the site)

Sources: https://epiisod.com/en-au/faqs (Booking and payment), https://epiisod.com/en-au/cancellation-policy, and the online booking pages.

## Steps
From the FAQ "How do I book a room?":
- Step 1: Select your preferred room type and tenancy duration on our website
- Step 2: Create an account with EPIISOD
- Step 3: Complete your application and pay the holding deposit
- Step 4: Acknowledge and sign-off on your tenancy agreement
- "At this stage, your room will be reserved."

The online booking journey is presented in three stages: "Profile Setup" (browse rooms and choose the one that feels right; "Tell Us" about yourself), "Room Reservation & Preferences" (choose room type, view and other preferences and "secure it instantly with a holding deposit"), and "Contract & Bond Payment" ("Review and acknowledge your contract, complete your bond payment, and set up direct debit for your recurring rent — you're almost ready to move in!").

You can filter rooms by preferred length of stay and check-in date, and request roommates (friends) in shared apartments by naming them in both applications (subject to availability). The application form asks for study details and lets you add special requests (including any medical requirements) or a promo/discount code. Final room allocations are subject to EPIISOD's discretion and may be adjusted if necessary; a similar room is assigned whenever possible. Residents "can select your desired room type, room facing and room level" (FAQ).

## Documents required
From the FAQ "What documents are required to book a room?":
- International students: a copy of your Student Visa (or confirmation that it is currently in process); your Confirmation of Enrolment (COE) from your university or college; a valid passport copy.
- Australian citizens or permanent residents: a valid passport or driver's license; an official enrolment letter from your university or college; your student ID card.
- (FAQ "Who can stay at EPIISOD?": Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) or an official Acceptance Letter; international students must also supply a copy of their student visa.)

## Holding deposit, bond and rent
- Holding deposit: "a 1-week deposit to reserve your room which will go towards your first rental payment."
- Bond: "a bond (equivalent to 4 weeks' rent) must be paid within 1 week of your deposit. This bond will be lodged with NSW Fair Trading and held as a security deposit for the duration of your stay." Refunded at the end of the tenancy provided there are no outstanding rent payments, damages to the room, or cleaning charges. (The online booking pages call it the "RTA bond"; the bond amount shown is "4 weeks rent".)
- Rent: "Subsequent rent will be charged every 2 weeks on the rental payment dates."
- Your reservation is only confirmed once you have received and signed your tenancy agreement.
- If your room is not available, the holding deposit and bond are refunded in full unless another room type is chosen (cancellation policy).
- Contracts are Residential Tenancy Agreements lodged with the NSW Civil & Administrative Tribunal (NCAT); non-payment is managed through a defined legal process and contracts are not automatically terminated (FAQ).
- When signing, the applicant acknowledges the Residential Tenancy Agreement, House Rules, Annexures and the EPIISOD Privacy Policy, and that they have access to the Tenant Information Statement published by NSW Fair Trading, and consents to receive notices and documents electronically.
- Applicants must be old enough: see faqs.md (age rules) - the booking form states "Under 18yo at start of tenancy is not allowed".
- Second occupant (Empowered Studio and Premier Empowered Studio at Macquarie Park): additional $60 per week; the second occupant must also be a student with a valid student ID.

## Payment methods (holding deposit)
- Credit card (VISA/MASTER/UNIONPAY) - "Credit card recommended: Instantly secures your booking right away."
- Bank transfer (Australian domestic remittance or international remittance): processing takes up to 3 working days ("Please be aware that your bank may charge a fee for this transfer (if it is international)"); you upload proof of payment (JPEG, JPG, PNG or PDF, max 10MB) and quote your reference. Verification of proof of payment may take up to 3 working days (another page states "Verification may take up to 5 working days").
- Alipay - processing takes up to 3 working days.
- Bond: paid through the RTA/bond process after the contract is acknowledged ("Submit your bond payment through the RTA to finalise your commitment"); "Please check back after 3 working days to accept the contract" may be shown while the bond payment is pending.
- Rent: set up by direct debit ("Set up automatic direct debit to make your ongoing payments simple, secure, and stress-free. With direct debit, your rent will always be paid on time, giving you peace of mind throughout your stay."). Paying by Direct Debit or approved bank transfer from an Australian bank account earns a 3% discount (see promotions.md). Students have a two-week grace period from the tenancy start date to set up an Australian bank account.
- Rent includes utility bills and high-speed Wi-Fi; laundry machines are pay-per-use.
- A selected room is held for a limited time while you complete the steps; the booking page shows a countdown, and if the hold time expires you must select the room again (the duration is not stated on the pages).
- After the holding deposit is paid you are asked to verify your email address so that your contract can be generated.
