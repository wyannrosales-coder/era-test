# Cancellation Policy (EPIISOD Rental Policy)

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/cancellation-policy

The page is titled "Epiisod Rental Policy / Cancellation Policy". It refers to "the state Residency Tenancy Act" (as written). The bond is lodged with NSW Fair Trading. Questions: use the Contact Us option on the page ("Feel free to reach out to us — whether you have a quick question, need assistance with your booking, or simply want to learn more, we're here and happy to help anytime!").

## 1. Booking Terms and Conditions

Upon payment of a holding deposit that is equivalent to one week's rent, signing of Tenancy Agreement and payment of bond that is equivalent to four week's rent, an applicant is consider having completed* the room booking with EPIISOD subjected to submission of required documents.

- The bond payment is lodged with the NSW Fair Trading in accordance with the state Residency Tenancy Act.
- The holding deposit paid will go towards the initial rent payment once the applicant checks into the property.
- If your room is not available, your holding deposit (if paid) and bond (if paid) will be refunded in full unless another room type is chosen.

*Relevant supporting documents must be submitted. Other terms and conditions may apply. For more details, please refer to our FAQ (https://epiisod.com/en-au/faqs?tab=1).

## 2. Cancellations received before or during the contract tenure indicated in the signed Residential Tenancy Agreement

2.1 All cancellation requests must be submitted using EPIISOD's Notice to leave form. This form can be requested from the reception or via email to EMPsydney@epiisod.com

2.2 All relevant fields in the Notice to leave form must be completed and submitted in-writing via e-mail to EMPsydney@epiisod.com

2.3 A notice period of minimum 14 days must be given to EPIISOD in accordance with the Residency Tenancy Act. As such, the earliest contract end date will be 14 days from the receipt of the cancellation request. Rent will be charged till this earliest date OR any later date requested by cancellation tenant.

2.4 Contract break fees will be levied in accordance with the state Residency Tenancy Act.

2.5 The contract break fee can be waived if cancellation tenant finds a qualified replacement tenant to assume the existing tenancy agreement. All replacements are subject to formal approval by EPIISOD. The replacement must be students from a higher education institution who does not hold a current tenancy agreement with EPIISOD. An administrative fee may apply to process this change.

2.6 The approved replacement tenant must complete the full booking process before the tenancy transfer is deemed official and the original tenant is released from their contract.

2.7 All promotions or incentives given as part of the contract will be voided due to uncompleted execution of the original tenancy contract. EPIISOD reserves the right to take back all promotion or incentives given to cancellation tenant.

2.8 Replacement tenant will not be allowed to continue enjoying the promotions or incentives attributed to the original booking.

2.9 EPIISOD reserves the right to reclaim costs incurred from cleaning premises occupied by a cancelling tenant that are not restored to their initial handover state. Pursuant to this policy, such costs may be deducted from the holding deposit or bond.

The site does not state the amount of the contract break fee or of the administrative fee for a replacement tenant (the break fee is "levied in accordance with the state Residency Tenancy Act").

## 3. Cancellation of Tenancy Agreement – Due to no Visa (International Students)

3.1 As an eligible international student (students coming from abroad), student can enter into a Residential Tenancy Agreement at EPIISOD, knowing that you have the option to terminate the tenancy agreement with limited liability under the following conditions:

- Full refund of holding deposit and bond
  - a. Written notification of visa rejection by the Department of Home Affairs must be provided to EPIISOD either
    - i. at least two (2) weeks before the contract start date OR
    - ii. within twenty-four (24) hours of receiving the visa rejection notification from the Department of Home Affairs (if rejection notification from department of Home Affairs occurs less than 2 weeks from tenancy start date)

3.2 A duly completed cancellation form is submitted to EPIISOD BEFORE the tenancy's start date.

3.3 Expiry of existing VISA does not fall within this condition as it is student's decision not to renew the VISA. In this case, break fees as stipulated in in accordance with the state Residency Tenancy Act applies

3.4 If visa status remains unconfirmed a week before the agreement start date, student must notify EPISOD of their current status. A deferral of up to one month may be granted, subject to approval. If visa status remains unconfirmed by the extended deferral start date, the tenancy will be deemed terminated by the applicant, and break fees will apply in accordance with the state Residential Tenancies Act.

(The property pages carry the banner "FULL REFUND FOR VISA REJECTION - T&C APPLIES", which links to this policy.)

## 4. Cancellation by EPIISOD

4.1 EPIISOD will cancel any incomplete application submitted via our online application portal that has past the given timeframe for completion. In such case, EPIISOD may charge an administrative fee and/or retain any holding deposit and/or any other amounts you have paid to us.

4.2 At any time during or prior to the commencement of your residential tenancy agreement, EPIISOD may (at our complete discretion, but otherwise to the extent permitted by the state Residency Tenancy Act) cancel your booking if any of the following occurs
- (a) the information you provide as part of the booking process is false or misleading; or
- (b) you fail to comply with your obligations in these Booking Terms and Conditions.

4.3 In this case, to the extent permitted by the state Residency Tenancy Act, we may retain any holding deposit and/or any other amounts you have paid to us.

## 5. Contract Deferment

5.1 The management team will review each deferment request on a case-by-case basis. Management reserves the right to approve or deny any deferment request based on the provided documentation and the merit of the case.

5.2 Students must submit a deferment request in writing at least 14 days before the tenancy commencement date with comprehensive supporting document.

5.3 The first deferment charges will be waived, but any subsequent changes will incur a $250 administrative fee which can be waive by the Management on a case-to-case basis.

# Privacy Policy

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/privacy-policy

Summary of the published policy (the site shows a short version and a longer numbered version). The policy is that of Centurion Student Services Pty Ltd (ACN 086 692 051) and its related businesses ("EPIISOD"). Last updated 22 January 2026.

## Who and what it covers
- EPIISOD provides student accommodation services and related products, including apps and platforms, and collects Personal Information to provide these services, improve them, and manage internal business operations.
- Primary obligations come from Australian law, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Where EU or UK residents or citizens are involved, the EU GDPR and UK GDPR also apply (additional rights apply, see below).
- "GBV Code" means the National Higher Education Code to Prevent and Respond to Gender based Violence 2025, which came into effect on 1 January 2026.

## Consent
- By providing Personal Information you consent to its collection, use, storage and disclosure in accordance with the policy or as required or permitted by law. If you continue using the products and services, that use is treated as consent.

## Information collected
- Name and contact details, including date of birth and address; gender identity; ID such as passport or driver's licence; student number and details of school, college or university and course of study; details of next of kin or emergency contacts; CV, employment history and education background (job applicants or staff); information about use of websites, applications and services (cookies, IP addresses, device ID, email subscription and membership details); information in communications between you and EPIISOD; payment information (such as bank account details).
- Sensitive information (only with consent or as permitted by law): health information, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, membership of associations, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual orientation or practices, and criminal record (for Australian individuals only).
- To comply with the GBV Code, EPIISOD may also collect demographic information (such as gender identity, Indigenous status, disability status, language used at home, sexual orientation, requirement for an interpreter), mode of attendance, citizenship status, accommodation status, and information relating to disclosures and formal reports of gender-based violence.
- Parent or guardian consent: parents or guardians who provide a dependant's information confirm they have the responsibility and legal capacity to consent on the dependant's behalf and have read the policy to them.
- You may choose not to identify yourself or use a pseudonym when you contact EPIISOD (for example to lodge a complaint), but products or services cannot be provided to someone who remains anonymous.

## How information is collected
- Forms; registering or using services on EPIISOD websites; interactions with staff; entering a contract or purchasing services; creating online accounts; contact requests; mailing lists; responding to promotions or surveys; job or scholarship applications; references from partners; browsing the website (cookies).

## How information is used
- To provide products and services; identify and interact with you; manage the relationship; improve services; update records; respond to queries and complaints; send marketing and publicity; security, access control and surveillance; handle disputes and investigations (including under the GBV Code); protect legal rights; prevent crime; comply with law; and any purpose you consent to. Specific purposes are listed for tenants/residents (due diligence checks, lease documentation, rent payments, complaints, preventing and responding to gender-based violence), app users, next of kin, vendors/contractors, job candidates and employees.

## Disclosure
- May be disclosed to EPIISOD entities, joint venture partners, service providers and advisers, property operators or managers, business partners, insurers, debt collection and dispute resolution services, auditors and lawyers, government regulators and law enforcement, banks and card companies, anyone you agree to, the Secretary of the Department of Education (under the GBV Code), and student accommodation providers where necessary to protect safety.

- Overseas: some recipients are based overseas, such as in the United Kingdom, the United States of America or Singapore; EPIISOD uses arrangements such as approved contractual clauses to ensure adequate protection.

## Direct marketing (Australian individuals)
- EPIISOD may contact you to promote products and services and may share information with commercial and business partners for marketing. You can opt out by emailing the DPO, through account settings, or via the unsubscribe link in each communication.

## Cookies and third-party sites

You can refuse via the cookie pop-up or change browser settings; some functions may not work without cookies. EPIISOD is not responsible for the privacy practices of linked third-party websites.

## Security and retention
- Personal Information is held in secure computer storage and paper files, with encryption, access restrictions, penetration testing, monitoring, staff training and physical and cyber security measures. A Data Protection Officer (DPO) oversees compliance. No internet security is risk-free, and you are encouraged to keep passwords confidential.

# Gender-Based Violence (GBV) Support

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/gender-based-violence-gbv-support

## Creating a Safe and Respectful Community
At EPIISOD, we are committed to fostering a safe, inclusive and respectful living environment where every resident feels valued, supported and free from violence, harassment, discrimination and abuse.

Gender-Based Violence (GBV) has no place in our communities. We encourage residents to seek support if they have experienced, witnessed or are concerned about any form of GBV.

No concern is too small. Support is available whether or not you choose to make a formal report.

## What is Gender-Based Violence?
Gender-Based Violence (GBV) refers to harmful behaviour directed at a person because of their gender, gender identity, gender expression or perceived gender.

GBV can take many forms, including:
- Sexual harassment
- Sexual assault
- Stalking
- Domestic or intimate partner violence
- Coercive or controlling behaviour
- Online harassment or cyber abuse
- Emotional or psychological abuse
- Discrimination based on gender

Everyone deserves to feel safe and respected in their accommodation community.

## Report a Concern
If you have experienced, witnessed or are concerned about Gender-Based Violence, we encourage you to report it.
- Report a Concern (online): https://gbv.epiisod.com/
- Email Our Team: gbv@epiisod.com

## What Happens Next?
When a report is submitted:
1. Your concern will be reviewed by the appropriate team.
2. Available support options will be discussed with you.
3. Information will be handled sensitively and confidentially wherever possible.
4. Appropriate follow-up actions will be taken.

## Emergency Support
If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000 immediately.

## Support Services
You do not need to make a report before accessing support services.

**Healthdirect Australia - 1800 022 222**
Free government-funded health advice from a registered nurse, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Healthdirect can help you understand medical concerns, access healthcare services and determine whether further medical treatment is required.

**1800RESPECT - 1800 737 732**
Australia's national sexual assault, domestic family violence and abuse support service. Provides free and confidential information, counselling and support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week via phone, online chat and other channels.
Best for: sexual assault; domestic and family violence; safety planning; emotional support; referrals to local services.

**MensLine Australia - 1300 789 978**
A free 24/7 counselling and referral service for men experiencing challenges relating to mental health, relationships, family violence, stress, separation and wellbeing. Professional counsellors provide confidential support and guidance.
Best for: men's mental health; relationship concerns; family violence support; stress and emotional wellbeing.

**QLife - 1800 184 527**
Free and anonymous LGBTIQ+ peer support and referral service for people wishing to discuss sexuality, gender, identity, relationships, wellbeing and related concerns. Available every day from 3pm–9pm (local time).
Best for: LGBTQIA+ support; identity and sexuality concerns; emotional support; community referrals.

## Our Commitment
We are committed to fostering safe, inclusive and respectful communities where all residents feel valued, supported and protected from Gender-Based Violence (GBV).

We maintain a zero-tolerance approach to violence, harassment, abuse and discrimination. Through education, awareness, support services and accessible reporting pathways, we aim to prevent GBV and respond appropriately when concerns are raised.

We encourage all residents to familiarise themselves with our GBV Policy and available support resources. If you have experienced, witnessed or are concerned about Gender-Based Violence, support is available and we encourage you to seek assistance.

Other safety support mentioned on the site: residents receive complimentary access to the Sonder app (24/7 safety check-ins, real-time support, and telehealth and mental health services); residential assistants are available onsite, even outside regular office hours; all entrances are monitored by 24/7 CCTV (see faqs.md).

# Whole-of-Organisation Prevention and Response Plan (summary)

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/whole-organisation-plan

Title on the page: "Whole-of-Organisation Plan". The plan is published by Dwell Student Living Pty Ltd (Dwell) and EPIISOD, brands under Centurion Corporation Ltd (an SGX-listed entity). The plan was updated in June 2026. It responds to the National Higher Education Code to Prevent and Respond to Gender-based Violence ("National Code"), Standard 7.

## 1. Statement of commitment from EPIISOD
- Dwell and EPIISOD provide far more than accommodation; they commit to a vibrant, supportive environment where students can thrive academically, socially and personally. The safety and wellbeing of residents and employees is the highest priority.
- Gender-based violence is unacceptable in any form; the organisation welcomes the National Code and supports its intent.
- Organisational values: respect, integrity, creativity and excellence. Commitment to continuous improvement by regularly reviewing policies, procedures and systems.

## 2. Introduction
- Dwell / EPIISOD provide purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA) in major cities; the portfolio is "rapidly expanding with new sites being added in 2027 onwards". Safety features include 24/7 security features, onsite support, and Resi-Life programs.
- The plan is structured around the five sections of Standard 7 of the National Code: Accountable leadership and governance (7.2 – 7.3); Safe environments and systems (7.4); Knowledge and capability (7.5 – 7.6); Safety and support (7.7); Data, evidence and impact (7.8). Each section has a table of mandatory requirements, key actions and evidence of compliance. Appendix A is the Whole-of-Organisation assessment; Appendix B lists the National Code Standards 7.2 to 7.8 with summary text.

## 3. Accountable leadership and governance (7.2 – 7.3)
- The plan is published on the websites (www.dwellstudent.com/en-au and www.epiisod.com/en-au) and was updated in June 2026.
- Residents were consulted when developing the plan; local leadership and Centurion headquarters implement it together.
- An evaluation framework (objectives, measures, indicators, data sources, analysis, incorporating learnings) is being developed; progress reports are provided to relevant universities on request; the plan is reviewed at least every four years.

## 4. Safe environments and systems (7.4)
- Staff must declare previous investigations or substantiated allegations of gender-based violence in similar roles and any existing or previous intimate personal relationship with a resident; risks are considered in employment decisions and managed. A screening form, a risk-consideration process and a conflict-of-interest policy are in place. Centurion requires all staff in Australia to have valid Working With Children Checks and National Police Checks and an annual conflict-of-interest declaration; a specialist HR firm supports management of issues.
- Non-disclosure agreements are not used with residents or employees (unless requested by the discloser); settlement agreements do not contain non-disparagement clauses.
- Dwell/EPIISOD has its own gender-based violence policy and procedure, and mechanisms for anonymous disclosures and formal reports through a QR code, website and app; resident induction packs include this information.

## 5. Knowledge and capability (7.5 – 7.6)
- Residents and staff complete prevention education and responding-to-disclosures training tailored to student accommodation; a needs assessment was conducted; a Reference Group of staff, residents and experts guides development; a specialist organisation develops and delivers training.
- Risk assessments are only undertaken by people with the required expertise; a specialist provider conducts risk assessments and upskills staff to triage risk after hours.

## 6. Safety and support (7.7)
- Responses and support services are safe, person-centred and trauma-informed. Support services are provided or facilitated for disclosers and respondents; partnerships with universities and external crisis services provide after-hours support; staff can access an EAP provider 24/7; support information will be published on the website or app.
- A risk assessment follows every disclosure and formal report. Where behaviour occurs in residence or at a residence event, necessary action is taken to manage risk, including relocation of the respondent and urgent access to support; emergency accommodation can be arranged for respondents.
- A resident discloser may choose the university or Dwell/EPIISOD to lead the response (policy and procedure clause 10.1). Where the accommodation provider leads: a risk assessment is undertaken within 48 hours with safety measures (including relocation) implemented immediately; risk is managed and monitored; necessary information is shared to protect others; support plans for disclosers and respondents are implemented within 48 hours; staff are trained to record incidents. Information-sharing agreements are in place with various universities.

## 7. Data, evidence and impact (7.8)
- All staff are trained to recognise forms of gender-based violence for uniform data entry (with a subject matter expert where needed); an incident reporting system captures the required data.
