## 1. Save 3% with Direct Debit

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/promotions/save-3-direct-debit-0

Short description: Pay via Direct Debit and enjoy discounted weekly rates.

Details: "A simpler way to pay, with a little extra saving built in. Pay your rent by Direct Debit from an Australian bank account and enjoy 3% off the standard weekly rent throughout your tenancy. Once it's set up, your payments happen automatically, so there's one less thing to think about."

Why pay by Direct Debit?
- 3% off your weekly rent
- Automatic, hassle-free payments
- No need to keep track of payment due dates
- Secure and easy to manage

Room-page summary: 3% off your weekly rent; discounted rate is reflected on the room listing; pay your rent via Direct Debit from an Australian bank account. (Room listings show the standard price and the discounted price, for example "3% off AU$747.42 AU$725.00 / wk - You save AU$22.42 every week".)

Terms & Conditions:
- The advertised discounted rent includes a 3% discount.
- The 3% discount applies when rent is paid via an approved electronic bank transfer funded from an Australian bank account, including Direct Debit.
- Students are given a two-week grace period from the commencement date of their tenancy to establish an Australian bank account. During this period, the discounted rent will continue to apply.
- If rent is paid using a payment method other than an approved electronic bank transfer, the discount will not apply to the relevant rent instalment(s), and the standard rent will be payable.
- The discount applies only to eligible rental charges and is not redeemable for cash or transferable.
- EPIISOD reserves the right to amend or withdraw this offer where required by law or operational requirements.

## 4. Refer a Friend - Double the Joy, $600 in Rewards!

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/promotions/refer-friend-double-joy-600-rewards

Short description: Refer a friend to EPIISOD and you'll both receive a $300 rent credit once they move in. It's our way of saying thanks for growing our community!

Overview: "At EPIISOD, we believe the best neighbors are the ones you choose yourself. When you refer a friend to join our community, we want to say thank you with a bonus that benefits both of you."

The Reward: "When your friend signs a lease with us, we'll apply a $300 rent credit to both your account and theirs. That's a total value of $600 back into your pockets!"
- Total Value: $600 ($300 each)
- Convenience: Applied directly to your resident accounts
- Transparent: All credits are inclusive of GST

How it Works (to ensure you both receive your credit):
1. Fill up and Submit the Form: Download and complete the referral form on this page (https://drive.google.com/file/d/110H8ejOM2cgehFJZ5ylJo-9HaMT9jsRU/view?usp=sharing). Or swing by the reception to pick up a copy. Fill it out with your details and your friend's, and hand it back to us or email us at empsydney@epiisod.com before they complete their booking.
2. Secure the Stay: Help your friend book a 48-week or 52-week lease. It's quick, easy, and just a few clicks away.
3. The New Resident Checklist: The referral reward is confirmed once your friend:
   - Signs a Residential Agreement for a minimum of 48 weeks.
   - Pays the required bond (4 weeks' fees).
   - Pays the holding deposit (1 week's fees).

What Happens Next? "Once your friend officially moves in, we'll apply a $300 credit to both of your accounts. No fine print, just real benefits. Sit back, relax, and enjoy your credit!"

The Essentials:
- Who is eligible? All current residents who refer a new face to our community.
- What's the catch? Just ensure they sign for a full academic year (48 or 52 weeks) and haven't lived with us before.
- The Bonus: Your credits are applied directly to your rent—non-transferable and non-refundable, but 100% helpful for your budget!

Terms & Conditions: "The referred friend must be a first-time EPIISOD resident. Both parties must sign a 48 or 52-week Residential Agreement. Credits are not redeemable for cash. This program cannot be combined with agent referrals. EPIISOD Macquarie reserves the right to modify or terminate this promotion at any time.

The referral credit will be issued during the third month of the referee's lease and applied to both the referrer's and referee's accounts. If the referrer has already moved out, the credit may be issued via bank transfer to their designated account."

Room-page version: "Refer a Friend & Both Save $300! Love living at EPIISOD? Share the experience! Refer a friend for a minimum 48-week stay, and we'll give both of you a $300 rent credit once they've signed their agreement and paid their bond. That's $600 in total rewards for our resident community." How to claim: (1) Grab the Form: pick up a 'Refer a Friend' form at reception, fill it out and hand it back before your friend completes their booking. (2) Secure the Stay: help your friend book their 48 or 52-week lease via our website. (3) Get Credited: once your friend moves in, the savings are applied directly to both accounts.
