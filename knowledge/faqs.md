# EPIISOD Frequently Asked Questions

Source: https://epiisod.com/en-au/faqs

Scope note: the FAQ answers on the site are written for EPIISOD Macquarie Park (Sydney, NSW). They mention NSW Fair Trading, NCAT and the Macquarie Park team contact details. The site does not publish a separate FAQ for EPIISOD Bedford (Melbourne). The property pages for Macquarie Park and Bedford do not contain their own FAQ sections.

## GENERAL

### 1. How do I check for room availability?

You may check for room availability on our website:

1. Select your desired room type
2. Under the ‘Find Your Room’ section of the room page, you can look through the room availability

### 2. Is the student population diverse?

Absolutely. At EPIISOD, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment. We actively embrace diversity and promote a culture of equality and fairness. Our team is trained to ensure all students and visitors are treated with dignity and respect.

Our goal is to create a home where everyone feels safe, supported, and free from discrimination, harassment, or bullying.

### 3. Who can stay at EPIISOD?

Our accommodation is available to students enrolled at a recognised educational institution. To complete your booking, you will need to provide a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) or an official Acceptance Letter. International students must also supply a copy of their student visa.

- International Students: Only those who have turned 18 on or before the tenancy start date are eligible.
- Domestic Students: Students must turn 18 within the calendar month in which the tenancy commences. Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate students who turn 18 later in the year.

### 4. I am under 18, can I live at EPIISOD?

Students must be 18 years old or turning 18 within two weeks of their check-in date, to be eligible to live at Epiisod.

### 5. How safe is our accommodation?

The safety and well-being of our residents is our top priority. All entrances to our properties are monitored by 24/7 CCTV, and each apartment is secured with a controlled access system.

Additionally, residential assistants are available onsite, even outside regular office hours, to provide support whenever needed. We also conduct regular fire alarm testing and adhere to strict fire safety protocols across all EPIISOD properties.

### 6. Can a formaldehyde test report be provided? Will the building have an official occupancy certificate?

Once the building has achieved an Occupancy Certificate (OC), it is deemed fit for occupation and compliant with the Australian Building Code. This process requires certification and approval by the relevant council and government authorities.

Following OC, Practical Completion (PC) is achieved and the building is formally handed over for operation.

### 7. Is there any obstruction to the view from the apartment?

The property is in a leafy area and is surrounded by nearby buildings. For more open views, we recommend selecting City View or Pool View rooms, which are generally located on higher floors.

## BOOKING AND PAYMENT

### 1. What documents are required to book a room?

To complete your booking, please provide the following documents based on your residency status:

**For International Students:**

- A copy of your Student Visa (or confirmation that it is currently in process).
- Your Confirmation of Enrolment (COE) from your university or college.
- A valid passport copy.

**For Australian Citizens or Permanent Residents:**

- A valid passport or driver’s license.
- An official enrolment letter from your university or college.
- Your student ID card.

If you need further assistance or clarification, our team is ready to help!

### 2. How do I book a room?

- Step 1: Select your preferred room type and tenancy duration on our website
- Step 2: Create an account with EPIISOD
- Step 3: Complete your application and pay the holding deposit
- Step 4: Acknowledge and sign-off on your tenancy agreement

At this stage, your room will be reserved.

### 3. What payments do I need to make when applying?

When you book, you are required to pay a 1-week deposit to reserve your room which will go towards your first rental payment. Additionally, a bond (equivalent to 4 weeks’ rent) must be paid within 1 week of your deposit. This bond will be lodged with NSW Fair Trading and held as a security deposit for the duration of your stay.

At the end of your tenancy, the bond will be refunded—provided there are no outstanding rent payments, damages to the room, or cleaning charges.

### 4. When do I pay my rent?

Subsequent rent will be charged every 2 weeks on the rental payment dates. For further assistance regarding rent payment, please contact our friendly staff:

- Email: empsydney@epiisod.com

- Hours: Weekdays 9am to 5pm Sydney Time (Exclude Public Holiday)

### 5. Is my reservation confirmed once I pay the holding deposit?

Your reservation is only confirmed once you have received and signed your tenancy agreement.

### 6. Can I view the accommodation before applying?

As the property is not yet ready, we are unable to provide tours of the actual building. However, you are welcome to visit our show flat, where we can walk you through the layout, furnishings, and facilities to give you a clear idea of what to expect.

To schedule a showflat tour, please contact our team:

- Email: empsydney@epiisod.com

### 7. What is included in my rent?

Your rent is all-inclusive, covering utility bills and high-speed Wi-Fi—so you’ll never need to worry about unexpected charges or paying bills on time.

This includes most of our exclusive Resi-Life Programme, offering a curated calendar of resident-only events and activities throughout the year. Many events are complimentary, though select experiences may incur an additional ticket fee.

The only additional cost is for the use of laundry machines, which are pay-per-use.

### 8. Will I be able to select my own desired room?

Yes. You can select your desired room type, room facing and room level.

### 9. Can I live with my friends or specify preferences for my roommates?

Yes! If you wish to live with friends in our shared apartments, please mention their names in your application and ensure they do the same in theirs. While we strive to accommodate roommate preferences, this is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed.

### 10. Can I have additional occupant staying with me throughout my lease?

At EPIISOD Macquarie Park, we allow another occupant to stay with you for an additional $60 per week, depending on your selected room:

- Empowered Studio
- Premier Empowered Studio

For more information, please contact our team at EPIISOD Macquarie Park:

- Phone: +61 (0) 417 977 706
- Hours: Weekdays 9am to 5pm Sydney Time (Exclude Public Holiday)

### 11. If I fail to make payment for an extended period, will my contract be automatically terminated?

No, contracts are not automatically terminated. I will be signing a Residential Tenancy Agreement that is lodged with the NSW Civil & Administrative Tribunal (NCAT). Any non-payment issues are managed through a clearly defined legal process, which may involve penalties.

### 12. What if I choose to stay at EPIISOD longer than my contract length?

Yes, you can stay at EPIISOD for the duration of your studies. We will contact you prior to the end of your lease to see if you would like to renew your contract with us.

### 5. Can I store my luggage before my lease starts?

No, complimentary luggage storage is not available prior to lease commencement.

Check out services like TAXIBOX (https://www.taxibox.com.au/). They are a convenient mobile storage solution—they deliver a storage unit to the site for you to load, then take it away to store securely. Prices start from around $199 per month.

### 6. Will I receive a checklist to inspect my apartment at check-in?

Yes. You will be required to complete a Condition Report within 24 hours of arrival as part of the Residential Tenancy Agreement. This must be returned to the EPIISOD Macquarie Park team.

### 7. What kind of electrical equipment can I use?

For safety, please observe the following guidelines:

- Only use electrical appliances purchased in Australia
- Do not overload power sockets
- Never leave electrical items plugged in unattended
- Kitchen appliances must not be used in bedrooms

You are responsible for ensuring all appliances you bring are safe, free from defects, and in good working condition. Appliances that are broken, have frayed/exposed wires, show signs of overheating, or have loose connections are prohibited. Unsafe items identified during inspections will be removed for your safety.

### 8. Can I bring my own refrigerator? Does the refrigerator include a freezer?

Each studio includes a refrigerator. Shared apartments have a shared refrigerator in the common kitchen. Personal refrigerators or large appliances are not permitted unless prior approval is obtained from management.

There is no standalone freezer. However, studio refrigerators include a small freezer or cold box compartment.

### 9. If I do not select the rice cooker or toaster during the booking phase, may I know which appliance I will receive?

If I book a studio without selecting a rice cooker or toaster, a toaster will be provided by default.

### 10. Can I keep pets at EPIISOD?

Pets are generally not permitted at EPIISOD. An exception is made for recognised assistance animals (e.g. a seeing or hearing guide dog). In all cases, residents must seek written approval from EPIISOD before bringing any animal onto the premises.

### 11. Is the shower fixed or handheld?

The shower is fixed.

### 12. What do I need to bring?

Our rooms come fully furnished, including a mattress and mattress protector. You will need to bring your own towels, blankets, sheets, and pillow.

Bed sizes are as follows:

- Studio apartments: Double bed
- Quad/Quint rooms: King single bed

Residents are required to supply their own kitchen items, including plates, cups, cookware, cutlery, and any additional cooking utensils. A toaster or rice cooker will be provided. You will also need to bring personal items such as coat hangers, cleaning products, laundry detergent, toiletries, and your own clothing and belongings.

## MOVE-OUT

### 1. When does my contract start or end in 2026/2027?

- Semester 1: Contracts may commence anytime between 7th February 2026 and 20th February 2026. The contract end date will be on or before 14th July 2026 or 16th February 2027, depending on your chosen lease duration.
- Semester 2: Start dates are yet to be confirmed. Stay updated on Semester 2 bookings and announcements by subscribing to our newsletter. The contract will conclude on or before 16th February 2027 or 14th July 2027, based on your lease term.

Please note: You may move in any time after your contract begins; however, rental payments are calculated from the contract start date. Do reach out to empsydney@epiisod.com for more details.

### 2. I want to leave before my contract ends. What can I do?

If you wish to vacate your accommodation prior to the contract end date, please review our cancellation policy (https://epiisod.com/en-au/cancellation-policy) carefully. It provides clear instructions on the necessary steps and any applicable fees.

### 3. How do I get my bond back?

At EPIISOD Macquarie Park, bond returns are initiated within 10 business days after you vacate the property. You will receive an email with a link to review the bond claim details. Confirm or dispute charges and provide your bank details for the refund. Once all parties agree, the Residential Bonds Office (RBO) will process the payment within a few business days.

Please note: Any damages beyond normal wear and tear may affect your bond refund.

### 4. Do I have to pay for the damages?

We strive to provide a comfortable living experience. Our on-site maintenance team promptly addresses all repair requests.

Charges will only apply for damages caused by misuse or neglect. Normal wear and tear are not chargeable. Residents are responsible for their guests’ conduct; any damages caused by guests will be charged to the hosting resident. If damage occurs in shared spaces and responsibility cannot be determined, costs will be split evenly among apartment residents.

All charges will be invoiced and payable within 30 days. For further inquiries, please contact our management team at empsydney@epiisod.com.
